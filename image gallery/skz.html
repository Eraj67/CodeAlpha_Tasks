Stary Kids

Stray Kids isn’t just a K-pop group—they’re a self-made powerhouse built on raw talent, creative control, and fearless individuality. Formed under JYP Entertainment, they stand out for producing and writing their own music, turning personal struggles, ambition, and identity into powerful sound. Their tracks hit with aggressive energy, deep emotion, and experimental beats that break the limits of typical K-pop. On stage, Stray Kids delivers unmatched intensity—every performance feels explosive, precise, and full of attitude. Their unit, 3RACHA, drives their musical identity, proving they don’t follow trends—they create them. With a global fandom, STAY, backing them, their influence keeps growing stronger. Stray Kids isn’t here to fit in—they’re here to lead, disrupt, and redefine what it means to be artists in a new generation.

CELINE

Celine is a French luxury fashion house founded in 1945 by designer Céline Vipiana. The headquarters are located at 16 rue Vivienne in the 2nd arrondissement of Paris at the Hôtel Colbert de Torcy, which has French Historic Monument classification.

HISTORY

In 1945, Celine Vipiana and her husband Richard created one of the first luxury brands in the industry. Celine began as a children's shoe brand and opened its first boutique at 52 rue Malte in Paris. The brand was recognized for its logo, the red elephant created by Raymond Peynet.

Creative Designers

Phoebe Philo, 2008–2018

In 2008, Bernard Arnault, the president of LVMH, selected Phoebe Philo to take on the role of creative director at Céline. She officially began in October of that year and introduced her debut ready-to-wear collection for the Spring/Summer 2010 season at Paris Fashion Week. Pierre-Yves Roussel shared that bringing Philo on board gave her space to share her creative vision. She left the brand in 2018 after nearly a decade of influence.

Hedi Slimane, 2018–2024

In early 2018, LVMH announced that Hedi Slimane would become the new artistic, creative, and image director of Céline. He introduced menswear, couture, and fragrances to the brand. He also updated the Celine logo and launched modern flagship stores in fashion capitals.

Michael Rider, 2025–Present

On October 2, 2024, LVMH announced that Michael Rider would become the new creative director of Celine, officially starting in 2025. He previously served as creative director at Ralph Lauren Polo and worked at Celine during Phoebe Philo’s time.

FASHION STYLE

Céline is known for its elegant and modern approach to fashion, focusing on clean lines, minimalism, and timeless pieces. Under Phoebe Philo, Céline became a symbol of “quiet luxury.” Later, Hedi Slimane brought a more youthful and edgy vibe to the brand.

FAMOUS SHOWS

Celine holds runway shows during Paris Fashion Week to present seasonal collections. One of the notable shows was the Spring-Summer 2011 collection under Phoebe Philo, which became popular for its minimalist designs.

ICONIC PRODUCTS

The Celine Luggage Tote and the Triomphe bag are two of the brand’s most famous handbags. The Triomphe bag is inspired by the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. Celine also creates sunglasses, perfumes, and stylish shoes.

STYLE AND DESIGN

Celine is known for its minimalist and chic designs. Under Hedi Slimane, the brand focused on clean cuts, neutral colors, and timeless pieces.

STORES WORLDWIDE

Celine has boutiques in major fashion capitals such as Paris, London, New York, Seoul, and Tokyo. Each store is designed with a modern and artistic interior.
